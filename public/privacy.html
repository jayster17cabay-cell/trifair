Privacy Policy

TriFair — Passenger Feedback System
Last updated: November 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how TriFair collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use the TriFair Passenger Feedback System ("the Service"). By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you use the Service:

  • Account information: name, email address, and password (hashed) when you register.
  • Complaint and violation reports: details you submit about transport incidents, including operator, location, date, and description.
  • Proof attachments: photos or documents you upload as evidence.
  • Device and usage data: basic technical information such as browser type and IP address, used for security and abuse prevention.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Operate and maintain the Service.
  • Verify your email address and authenticate your account.
  • Process, review, and resolve complaints and violation reports.
  • Notify you by email about the status of your reports.
  • Improve the Service and prevent fraud or abuse.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Transport operators and TODA officers involved in a complaint you filed, limited to what is necessary to resolve it.
  • TFRB officers and superadmins for review and enforcement.
  • Service providers that help us operate the Service (for example, email delivery), bound by confidentiality.
  • Legal authorities when required by law.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including password hashing and authenticated access controls. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

7. Cookies

The Service uses session cookies to keep you signed in and to maintain security. These are essential for the Service to function.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
